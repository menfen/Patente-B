Privacy Policy

App: Patente B (Android package com.patenteb.english, Windows and web versions). Effective date: 30 October 2026.

Summary

This app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and the Android app does not request internet access.

Data that stays on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves on your device only: your answers to practice questions, mock-exam results, the questions you have seen, and your settings (language, text size, sound). This data never leaves the device through the app. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot see it.

Text-to-speech

When you use the 🔊 buttons or narration, the text is passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google Speech Services). The app sends it nothing else. Depending on its own settings, that engine may process text according to its provider's privacy policy.

Permissions

The Android app requests no dangerous permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage). It only declares that it wants to find the device's text-to-speech service.

Links

Links in the app (for example to the source projects or official ministry websites) open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is a study aid for the driving-licence theory exam and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we process no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All data is under your control on your device. You can still contact us with any question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Developer: Mahmoud – houdah263@gmail.com. Security issues can be reported to the same address (see security.html).
