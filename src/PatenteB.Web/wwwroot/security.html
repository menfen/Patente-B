Security and vulnerability reporting

If you find a security problem in Patente B, please email houdah263@gmail.com with the subject "Security". Describe the problem, the app version (Settings) and the device. Please do not publish details until a fix is out.

We will confirm receipt within 5 working days, assess the report, and ship a fix through Google Play as an app update as soon as possible. Fixed issues are listed in the release notes. Support period: security updates are provided until at least 31 December 2031.

Design: the Android app has no internet permission, no accounts and no server; all content is packaged in the app and your progress stays on your device. The list of third-party components is in licenses.html.
